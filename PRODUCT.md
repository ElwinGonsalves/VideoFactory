# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary: a solo creator** running a faceless explainer channel (YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok) from one Windows laptop (RTX 4050, 6 GB VRAM, often 2-3 GB RAM free). They write or paste a script, render, check facts and QA frames, and stagger uploads overnight, often late in the evening (inferred from the scheduler defaults "Tonight 11:00 PM" and keep-awake).
- **Secondary: Kiroween 2026 hackathon judges** (Frankenstein category) who clone the repo or open the running app and have about two minutes, possibly without a Gemini API key (from the owner's brief).

## Product Purpose

video-factory turns an idea, a pasted script, a spreadsheet row or a reference video into a finished, captioned, music-ducked H.264/AAC MP4 with thumbnail and upload metadata, then publishes it on a schedule. Success: a creator queues a week of Shorts in one sitting and wakes up to published videos with nothing held for unexplained reasons.

## Positioning

A Frankenstein of open-source organs stitched into one body that runs locally: Remotion (render), FFmpeg (mix, encode), faster-whisper (captions), Edge-TTS / Kokoro (voice), Explainroo and Manim (alternate engines), the watch-video analyzer (reference DNA), Playwright (cookie uploads). Only the language model (Gemini free tier by default, Ollama offline) is remote. No stock-footage or paid media APIs.

## Operating Context

- Runs at `http://127.0.0.1:8765` with a per-launch token; the owner may keep it running while developing.
- Long jobs: a render takes about 3 minutes and renders run strictly one at a time.
- The scheduler daemon, `state.sqlite` and `data/projects.db` share state with the UI. Files live in `output/<project_id>/`.
- Project currently sits inside OneDrive (known locking risk, documented).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Six jobs, one destination each: Create (From Idea / From Script), Ideas (brainstorm, reference DNA / Style Blueprints, pitch cards), Batch (xlsx/csv/Google Sheets), Library (inspect, fact-check, download, re-render, publish), Schedule (pre-flight, builder, queue), Settings (model and spend, defaults, accounts, alerts, system).
- Safe-Publish Gate holds videos with unverified facts as `needs_review`. Gemini free tier has no Google Search grounding.
- Secrets stay in `.env` / `config/`; the browser only sees masked values.
- The legacy vanilla UI stays reachable at `/classic` (owner decision, 2026-11-23).
- Judge demo video is submitted separately; no rendered video is committed to the repo (owner decision, 2026-11-23).

## Product Principles

1. Show the machine working: every long job exposes its stage, the engine doing it, and time elapsed.
2. Never publish something the creator would not stand behind: facts and QA are visible before any upload.
3. Local first and honest: nothing claims to be live or done unless the backend says so.
4. Two minutes to understand: a first-timer or judge reaches a meaningful result without reading docs.
